Helping families stay connected during epilepsy monitoring.

CONTINUOUS MONITORING

Continuous monitoring

A lightweight headband with a brain-signal sensor continuously monitors the patient’s brain activity. A reference electrode sits behind the ear. The EEG view can show Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta and Theta waves.
