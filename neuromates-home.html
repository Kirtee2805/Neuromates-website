Advancing connected care for neurological health.

Neuro Mates is an AI and IoT-based smart care system designed to support people living with Parkinson’s, Alzheimer’s, Epilepsy and Paralysis through continuous monitoring, personalised rehabilitation, medication and activity support, and connected care for patients, caretakers and doctors.

Neurological care extends beyond the clinic.

Patients living with neurological conditions may require support long after a clinical appointment ends. Monitoring daily activities, remembering medication, maintaining rehabilitation routines, responding to emergencies and keeping doctors informed can place a significant burden on patients and their families.

Neuro Mates brings these elements together through a connected care platform designed to support patients, caretakers and doctors throughout the care journey.

HOW IT WORKS

From clinical recommendation to connected care.

Neuro Mates connects the people involved in a patient’s care through a structured referral and patient management system.

  1. 01Doctor recommends Neuro Mates

    A doctor introduces the appropriate Neuro Mates product based on the patient’s care requirements.

  2. 02Patient receives a referral code

    The doctor provides a unique referral ID for the patient registration process.

  3. 03Patient profile is created

    The patient receives a unique identity within the Neuro Mates Platform and is connected to the relevant disease-specific care environment.

  4. 04Product and monitoring begin

    The appropriate Neuro Mates product and connected devices can be used according to the patient’s care requirements.

  5. 05Platform receives relevant information

    Relevant monitoring, activity and care information reaches the connected software environment.

  6. 06Four portals keep the ecosystem connected

    Patient, Caretaker, Doctor and Admin portals provide role-specific access to the appropriate information and functions.

  7. 07Alerts and care actions happen

    Relevant alerts, reminders, reports and care information are communicated through the appropriate channels.

  8. 08Monitoring continues

    The system continues supporting the patient’s ongoing care journey.

Built with patient safety in mind.

Healthcare technology must be designed with reliability, privacy and responsible use of patient information in mind. Neuro Mates considers sensor validation, connectivity limitations, patient-specific AI performance and secure role-based access as important parts of the system.

  • Sensor ValidationMultiple checks can help reduce false alerts.
  • Data SecurityRole-based access separates patient, caretaker, doctor and admin information/functions.
  • Reliable ConnectivityData can be stored locally and synchronised when connectivity is restored.
  • Patient-Specific IntelligenceAI and ML models can be improved through patient-specific data and testing.

Technology informed by healthcare needs.

Neuro Mates is being developed with an understanding of real-world neurological care challenges and the role of wearable sensing, AI, IoT and remote healthcare technologies. Medical guidance has included consultation with neurological specialists to understand patient-care challenges, monitoring requirements and medical considerations.
