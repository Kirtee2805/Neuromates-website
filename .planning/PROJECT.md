# Project: Neuro Mates Website

## What This Project Is
Neuro Mates is a marketing and product showcase website for an AI and IoT-based neurological care ecosystem supporting Parkinson's, Alzheimer's, Epilepsy, and Paralysis. It communicates hardware sensing, cloud intelligence, and role-based portals (Patient, Caretaker, Doctor, Admin).

## Core Requirements & Deliverables
- **Home Page**: 11 sections covering Hero, Introduction, Our Products, How It Works, Four Portals, Technology, Proof of Concept Video, Trust & Safety, Medical & Research Foundation, Impact, and CTA.
- **5 Product Pages / Showcase**:
  - Products Overview
  - Parkinson's Care (Tremor/fall detection, interactive fall simulation, rehab)
  - Alzheimer's Care (Daily activity timeline, memory quiz, 1 km safe-zone geofence demo)
  - Epilepsy Care (EEG headband, abnormal signal alert flow, seizure diary)
  - Paralysis Care (BioAmp EMG/muscle tracking, gamified rehabilitation)
- **Technology Pages**: Overview, Connected Devices, AI & ML, Monitoring & Analytics, Connected Care Platform.
- **Insights & Research**: Overview, Research References, Empty-state Articles, Case Studies, Resources.
- **About Us & Contact**: About, Vision & Mission, Our Approach, Impact, Contact Us, Register for Demo.
- **3D Animation Experience**:
  - Glassmorphic / bioluminescent 3D neural brain model with interactive pulse and scroll parallax.
  - Exploded 3D wearable hardware view demonstrating sensor architecture and casing.
  - Floating 3D connected ecosystem (laptop + smartphones with dynamic notification overlays).

## Tech Stack & Standards
- HTML5, Modern Semantic CSS3, Vanilla ES6+ JavaScript.
- Three.js / WebGL for 3D interactive graphics.
- Fonts: `Fraunces` (Editorial Display) & `DM Sans` (Clean Body).
- Color System: Sage / Warm Cream background (`#F6F4EE`), Deep Ink (`#0E3B43`), Teal Accent (`#0B7A7C`), Emerald Green (`#2BB58A`), Cyan Sky (`#6EC1F0`), Coral Alert (`#FF7A6B`).

## Persistent Assets
- `assets/images/`: 15 high-res product and UI mockup assets from the specification.
- `assets/reference/`: Reference 3D video (`reference_3d_animation.mp4`), official copy doc (`.docx`), and original image collection (`.pdf`).
- `.planning/MEMORY.md`: Detailed specifications, section-by-section copy, and guidelines.
